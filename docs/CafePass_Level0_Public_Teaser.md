# CafePass

## One membership. A connected network of independent specialty cafés.

**Dubai, UAE · Pre-launch · Consumer membership platform**  
**Stage:** Pre-seed / Pre-launch  
**Sector:** Consumer subscriptions · Local commerce · Hospitality  
**Seeking:** USD 500,000 pre-seed  
**Use of funds:** Dubai pilot, café network build-out, product refinement, and 18-month execution runway

## The opportunity

Dubai has a strong and growing specialty-coffee culture, but independent cafés compete in a crowded market where customer acquisition is expensive and repeat visits are difficult to predict.

CafePass is building a single digital membership that connects coffee lovers with a curated network of independent specialty cafés across Dubai.

Members get one simple pass for discovering and visiting participating cafés. Participating cafés get a new channel for recurring foot traffic, discovery, and customer loyalty.

## What CafePass does

- **For members:** One membership to discover and visit a curated network of independent specialty cafés.
- **For cafés:** A digital channel to attract new customers, encourage repeat visits, and promote a café to a targeted local audience.
- **For the market:** A connected discovery and loyalty layer for independent coffee, rather than another discount-led marketplace.

## Why now

- Dubai’s specialty-coffee scene is highly active, but discovery remains fragmented.
- Independent cafés need affordable, recurring customer acquisition.
- Consumers increasingly value curated local experiences and simple subscription access.
- Mobile-first payments, QR-based redemption, and digital membership infrastructure make a city-level coffee pass practical.

## Current status

CafePass is a **pre-launch** venture. Core platform flows and product foundation have been developed, and the company is preparing a controlled Dubai-first pilot. It does not yet claim participating cafés, paying members, revenue, retention data, or product-market fit.

## The plan

1. Select an initial Dubai catchment area.
2. Onboard a focused group of independent specialty cafés.
3. Launch a limited paid-member cohort.
4. Measure activation, redemption frequency, repeat usage, retention, and café value.
5. Refine pricing, café economics, product experience, and acquisition strategy.
6. Expand across Dubai only after the model shows repeatable demand and sustainable café participation.

## Investment opportunity

CafePass is raising a **USD 500,000 pre-seed round** to fund an 18-month Dubai execution and validation plan, including pilot launch, café onboarding, product refinement, member acquisition, café success, legal/compliance, UAE operational setup, working capital, and team execution.
